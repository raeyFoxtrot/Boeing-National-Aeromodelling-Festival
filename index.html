Boeing National Aeromodelling Festival
2027

28th–29th November 2026• IIT Kanpur

A two-day national festival of design, building and flight, organised by the Aeromodelling Club, IIT Kanpur in association Techkriti'27. Join students from across the country to learn aeromodelling from the ground up.

Certification

All participants receive official certificates from Boeing and IIT Kanpur.

Free of cost

There is no registration fee. Accommodation and food are provided at IIT Kanpur (terms and conditions apply).

Take-away kit

Take home an RC plane kit of your own (terms and conditions apply).

How to participate

  1. 1

    Form a team

    Bring together 4–5 engineering students. Students from any engineering department are eligible.

  2. 2

    Submit your application

    Complete the registration form with your team’s idea before 10th November 2026.

  3. 3

    Get selected

    Teams are selected on the basis of the application form.

  4. 4

    Build and fly

    Join us at IIT Kanpur on 28th–29th November 2026 for lectures, hands-on building and flying.

Applications are open

Form a team of 4–5 engineering/science students and submit your idea.
Teams are selected on the basis of the application form.
