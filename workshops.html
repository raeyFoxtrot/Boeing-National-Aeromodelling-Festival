The workshop introduces you to the festival and to the field of aeromodelling and systems engineering. It combines introductory lectures from professors with hands-on experience of building an aircraft model and flying it.

What to expect at IIT Kanpur

  • Understand the event

    Learn how the event is structured, what you can expect to gain from taking part, and how to participate. A clear picture of its structure and overall scope helps your team prepare well.

  • No prior experience required

    The workshop opens aeromodelling to students from every stream and background. You do not need any prior experience to join.

  • Explore the competition and RC glider design

    Get a detailed walkthrough of the competition problem statement, along with a presentation on the design of an RC glider.

  • Learn aircraft design and modelling

    If you want to understand how modern aircraft are designed and modelled, this workshop offers a strong foundation and a significant learning experience.

The workshops also include a networking session with aeromodelling enthusiasts from across the country. We look forward to welcoming aspiring aeromodeller in large numbers.

Workshop Timeline

The workshop runs across 28th and 29th November 2026 at IIT Kanpur. Participants with confirmed selection are accommodated from the night of 27th November.

  1. 27th November 2026

    Arrival

    • Participants move into their allocated stays.
    • Dinner is served at the allocated stay.

  2. 28th November 2026

    Day 1

    • Breakfast at the allocated stay.
    • The workshop begins early in the morning.
    • Lunch at the workshop venue.
    • Sessions continue until the evening.
    • Participants then move to their allocated stays, where dinner is served.

  3. 29th November 2026

    Day 2 and departure

    • Breakfast at the allocated stay.
    • The workshop resumes early in the morning.
    • Lunch at the workshop venue.
    • The workshop concludes in the early evening.
